❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start playing?

Simply open the app and tap "Quick Match" to jump into a game immediately. You can also practice with specific sports or create custom games from the home screen.

How does the ranking system work?

Your ELO rating increases when you win matches and decreases when you lose. The amount of change depends on your opponent's rating. Win against higher-ranked players to climb faster!

What are the space badges?

As you level up, you unlock themed space badges representing your journey from a Small Rocket (Level 1) all the way to the Universe (Level 60). Each badge features unique designs that get more impressive as you progress.

How do daily streaks work?

Play at least one game each day to maintain your streak. Longer streaks unlock better daily rewards including XP boosts and bonus points!

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". This will restore any previous in-app purchases associated with your Apple ID.

The app is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try these steps: 1) Force close and reopen the app, 2) Check for app updates in the App Store, 3) Restart your device, 4) Reinstall the app. If issues persist, contact us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I delete my account?

To request account deletion, please email us at sulmusic2@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days and confirm once your data has been deleted.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Connection Issues

  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection (WiFi or cellular data)
  • Check if other apps can connect to the internet
  • Try switching between WiFi and cellular data
  • Disable VPN if you have one enabled

Game Performance

  • Close other apps running in the background
  • Make sure your device has sufficient storage space
  • Keep your iOS updated to the latest version
  • Restart your device if performance seems slow

Account & Progress

  • Your progress is saved automatically as you play
  • Sign in with the same account to sync across devices
  • Contact support if you notice missing progress or achievements
